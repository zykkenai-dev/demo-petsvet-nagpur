What a visit to Pet'sVet looks like.

“Dedicated pets clinic where you will get best clinical examination and medicines for your pets.” That's the promise, kept simple.

A proper exam

Head to tail, on a freshly cleaned table. Dr. Rode checks properly and explains what he finds in plain words.

Only what's needed

No scary words, no extra visits for the sake of it. If it's just ear wax, you'll hear “it's just ear wax.”

Medicines on hand

Pick up prescribed medicines, shampoos and care products right at the clinic, before you head home.
